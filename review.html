1. Letter 'ड' (डमरू) Comparison

Current (Half Diction) Abrupt 'd'
"डमरू वाला ड"

Final 'ड' sounds like 'ड्' (half diction, missing the ending vowel).

Recommended (Full Diction) Open 'da'
"डमरू वाला डा"

Ends in maatra 'a' (ा) for complete, resonant pronunciation of 'da'.

Alternative Nursery Format
"ड से डमरू"
🍎

2. Letter 'अ' (अनार) Samples

Option 1: Standard Vowel 'a'
"अनार वाला अ"

Natural vowel termination (अ is a pure vowel, no schwa deletion issue).

Option 2: Primer Format Traditional
"अ से अनार"

Standard Indian elementary textbook cadence (अ से अनार, आ से आम).

🪷

3. Letter 'क' (कमल) Comparison

Current (Half Diction) Abrupt 'k'
"कमल वाला क"

Audio clips off at 'क्' due to Hindi trailing schwa deletion.

Recommended (Full Diction) Open 'ka'
"कमल वाला का"

Ends in maatra 'a' (ा) for complete, open 'ka' sound.
