🛡️ Stylus & Palm Protection Guide

🖐️ 1. Software Palm Guard (Active on Slate)

The bottom translucent band on the blackboard is a protected Hand Rest Pad. Your child can comfortably rest their entire hand and wrist on it while writing above! Drag the ↕ Drag handle up or down to adjust its height.

⛶ 2. Hide Tablet Home & Back Buttons (Fullscreen Mode)

Tap ⛶ Fullscreen in the top toolbar. On Android tablets and Chrome, this completely hides the bottom Home, Back, and Recent Apps buttons so small hands cannot accidentally trigger them.

🔒 3. Child Lock for Tablets (Zero Accidental Exits)
  • iPad (Guided Access): Go to Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access (Turn ON). Inside Rasari, triple-click the Top/Power button to lock! Completely disables Home bar, gestures & Mission Control.
  • Android (App Pinning): Go to Settings → Security → App Pinning (Turn ON). Open Recents, tap the Rasari icon, and tap Pin. Locks the screen so Home & Back buttons cannot exit.
